## Public reconstruction studies read for this brief

**S7 — Andy Milburn, GHERKIN REVISITED, 3 February 2014.**
https://grevity.blogspot.com/2014/02/gherkin-revisited.html
A Revit teaching reconstruction: separates building systems and explains rotational
relationships. Its half-surface grid settings must not be treated as whole-building counts.

**S8 — Andy Milburn, GHERKINS FOR ALL, 16 February 2014.**
https://grevity.blogspot.com/2014/02/gherkins-for-all.html
Explains the repeating A-frame approach. The author explicitly treats 4.2-metre story
spacing and half-metre shell offsets as teaching approximations, not accurate dimensions.
The page advertises geometry sheets and Revit downloads. The geometry-sheet links
could not be retrieved; those attachments are NOT included in this pack.

**S9 — Brandon, Gherkin in Parametrics: The Process, 23 March 2011.**
https://b-john11.blogspot.com/2011/03/process.html
Documents exploration of radius/rotation relationships for Revit. It is another
reconstruction study, not original project geometry or proof of Milne's source selection.

Andy Milburn and Stuart Milne are different people. S7-S9 are verified examples of
public reconstruction material, not verified members of the demonstrator's collection.
