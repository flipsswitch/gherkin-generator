# Interior evidence and accuracy contract

Status: 17 October 2026. No new web research or proprietary building plans were used for this version. The original supplied source register remains [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md). It is not evidence for specific interior placements.

| Class | What it supports in this version |
| --- | --- |
| Sourced building facts | Original height, six lightwells and published 40-storey description, with the same limitations recorded in the original sources |
| Public reconstruction conventions | Original rotational relationship and 4.2 m teaching module |
| Original approximate parameters | Envelope radii, exact slab schedule, recesses, diagrid sizes, facade sampling and appearance |
| New interior assumptions | All core, lift, stair, room, door, furniture, plant and pipe positions, sizes, quantities, heights, routes and level assignments |
| Verified computational behavior | What the supplied tests and native read-back reports actually measure; this is evidence about the generated model, not the building |

Each generated interior component carries `evidence: assumed` in its mapping. The saved Blender scene also identifies its conceptual status. Model-level indices are deliberately kept separate from real storey names.

The two stairs and six lifts are choices for this experiment, not researched counts. Plant equipment represents a possible heating source, air-handling equipment, a tank and a pump set. It does not identify installed Gherkin equipment. Drain risers have no designed waste branches or below-ground connection. Fire and water mains have unresolved external supplies, pressure zones, isolation and backflow arrangements. Office layouts do not represent a particular tenant or date.

The concept preserves the original approximate envelope instead of silently claiming that more internal detail improves survey accuracy. Its core arrangement, column locations and wall construction do not establish a structural load path. Continuous internal columns, the original external diagrid and slabs are visible, but beam framing, connections, reinforcement, foundations and analysis are incomplete.

To replace assumptions with actual geometry, the next version needs authorized, dated and dimensioned plans for the core, stairs/lifts and each varying floor; sections and slab/shaft schedules; the relevant tenant fit-out drawings; coordinated mechanical, plumbing and fire-protection drawings; and a verified common datum. Each imported dimension should cite a document identifier, revision, page, unit and evidence class. A public leasing plan may establish one floor's advertised layout but cannot establish concealed service routes or other tenants' interiors.

Do not relicense third-party plans, photographs or drawings under this project's Unlicense without permission. The repository contains original procedural code and assumptions only. Engineering or construction use would require replacing those assumptions and completing the appropriate professional design and coordination work.
